One browser, synthetic loopback data, unchanged actual source producer. Four uninstrumented anchor requests precede and follow the matrix. Each of two owned instrumented pages runs A/B/A2 with six requests per block: A/A2 retain no response objects, B strongly retains Response + reader + AbortSignal until native terminal and body observation settle. Total: 44 ordinary requests, three negative controls; no rate/general reliability claim.

Both pages have identical scalar-only event logs and WeakMaps. Instrumentation observes fetch resolution, reader read/done/bytes/rejection/closed, release/cancel and AbortController.abort without suppressing them; `read()` returns its original promise. Strong retention is explicit and checked before release. Passive CDP sessions and request-finished body observers remain identical throughout. Record server hits/finish and independent expected payload digests, page monotonic timestamps, native terminal events, actual browser version and driver/source/runtime hashes. Negatives: real route.abort must fail before reaching the data endpoint; an explicit page AbortController abort on a still-open stream must be observed by signal/read/terminal instrumentation; a deliberately changed response payload must fail the independent expected-body comparator. These are separately labelled and excluded from ordinary matrix counts. Native action limits are 10 seconds, terminal/body settlement 5 seconds each, browser launch 60 seconds, whole experiment watchdog 180 seconds plus bounded teardown. Preserve preparation/execution failures; no rerun merely for green.

Decision rules: failure with verified strong references and reader.done rejects insufficient lifetime retention as a necessary explanation for that failure; failure with gate OFF rejects temporary interception as necessary. A small all-green cell alone establishes neither cause nor fix.
